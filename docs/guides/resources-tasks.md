# Resources tasks: how it works

This is the Resources department's to-do board on the website. You (Maeve) add tasks, your team claims them or gets them assigned, they hand in their work, you approve it, and approved work earns **stars**. Every **5 stars** earns a certificate.

**Where to find it:** sign in at [ralevel.com](https://ralevel.com) and click **Tasks** in the top menu. In the left sidebar, open **Departments → Resources**.

---

## The basics

- **Lists** hold tasks, for example "Maths notes" or "Past paper links". You can split a list into **sections**, for example "IGCSE" and "A Level".
- **Assignee** is the person doing the task. A task can have one assignee or none.
- **Reward** is how many stars a task is worth: none, ★1, ★2 or ★3.
- **Open for claiming** lets anyone in Resources take the task themselves.
- **My Tasks** in the sidebar shows everything assigned to you, wherever it is.
- Press **Q** anywhere in Tasks to quickly add a task.

## For Maeve (head)

### 1. Make a list
On the Resources page, click **New list**, give it a name and a colour, then add tasks to it.

### 2. Add a task
Type it into the list, or press **Q**. Click a task to open its details. There you can set:
- **Assignee**, if you already know who should do it.
- **Due** date and **Priority**.
- **Reward**: how many stars it's worth. Use more stars for bigger jobs, for example ★1 to fix a few broken links and ★3 for a full set of topical notes.
- **Claiming**: switch on **Open for claiming** to let your team pick it up themselves.

To find tasks with no one on them, look for the **Open** label. The ones open for claiming also appear under **Up for grabs** on the Resources page.

> Rewards and claiming only work on one-off tasks in a department list. Repeating tasks and Inbox tasks can't have them.

### 3. Review submitted work
When someone hands in a task with a reward, you get a Discord DM, and the task appears under **Waiting for review** on the Resources page.

Open the task to see their link and note, then either:
- click **Approve**: the task is done and they get the stars, or
- click **Request changes** and say what to fix. Your comment is added to the task, they get a DM, and they can submit again.

Ticking the checkbox on a task with a reward also approves it.

### 4. Certificates
When someone reaches 5 stars, and again at 10, 15 and so on, you and the owner get a DM. The person shows **Certificate due** in the **Team** table at the bottom of the Resources page.

An admin issues the certificate and then clicks **Mark issued**, which clears the badge. Their next certificate comes after 5 more stars.

### Good to know
- **You can't approve your own task.** If you claim or take a task with a reward yourself, an admin approves it.
- **Reopening takes the stars back.** If you approved something by mistake, untick it. The stars are removed and the task reopens. If it had been handed in, it goes back to "Waiting for review". To change the reward on an approved task, reopen it first.
- **Changing your mind:**
  - Setting the reward back to **None** turns it into a normal task, which the person just ticks off.
  - Reassigning a task clears the old hand-in.
- **The Team table** shows each person's open and overdue tasks, what they finished this week, and their stars.

## For the team (please share this part)

### Find work
Go to **Tasks → Resources**. Tasks under **Up for grabs** are free to take. Click **Claim** and it's yours. If someone claims it first, you'll see who has it.

Tasks Maeve assigns to you show up in **My Tasks**.

### Do it and hand it in
- **Task without a reward:** tick the checkbox when you're done.
- **Task with a reward (★):** tick the checkbox or click **Submit**. You can add:
  - a **link to your work**, such as a Google Drive file or folder
  - a short **note** for Maeve

  It then shows **In review** until Maeve looks at it.

### If changes are requested
You get a Discord DM, and the task shows **Changes requested**. Read Maeve's comment on the task, fix it, then click **Submit again**.

### Can't do it after all?
Open the task and click **Hand it back so someone else can claim it**. This works until you've submitted it.

### Your stars
Once you've earned some, your stars show on the Resources page (for example "★ 3 · 2 more to your certificate") and at the top of **My Tasks**. Every 5 stars earns a certificate. Maeve and the admins are told automatically when you reach one.

## Discord messages
The bot DMs you when:
- someone assigns you a task
- your work is approved (with your star count) or sent back with changes
- you have tasks due today or overdue: a short digest every morning at 9:00 IST
- **(Maeve only)** a task is ready for review, or someone is due a certificate

Not getting DMs? Ask an admin to add your Discord ID to your staff profile, and make sure your server privacy settings allow DMs from server members.
